KelbiTV Studio - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

KelbiTV Studio ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the KelbiTV channel to upload the channel's own animated videos to the channel's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app uses

  • Login tokens issued by YouTube (Google), TikTok and Instagram (Meta) after the channel owner signs in. They are used only to upload videos and read the basic name of the connected account.
  • The channel's own video files, titles and descriptions, which are stored on the owner's computer.

How the data is stored

All tokens and files stay on the owner's private computer. The app has no server, no database in the cloud and no analytics. Nothing is sold, shared or sent anywhere except to the platform the owner chooses to upload a video to.

Data of other people

The app does not collect, read or store any data about viewers or other users of YouTube, TikTok or Instagram.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect any account at any time in the app, and can also revoke access in the account settings of Google, TikTok or Meta. Disconnecting deletes the stored token.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube uploads are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the KelbiTV channel through its YouTube page youtube.com/@kelbi-tv.
